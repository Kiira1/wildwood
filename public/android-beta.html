Join the Android closed beta

Anyone with an eligible Google Play account and compatible Android device can join through our Google Group. Use the same Google account for the Group, the testing page, and your phone’s Play Store.

1. Join the Google Group

Open the Group and select Join group. Choose your email preference and confirm joining. You can choose “No email” and still participate.

Join the Google Group

No Join button? Sign in and check the account shown at the top right. If you see My membership settings, you are already a member and can continue to step 2. “You don’t have permission to access this content” refers to conversations; look for Join group above that message.

2. Opt in on Google Play

Choose Become a tester. If the page already says You are a tester, stay enrolled; do not leave just to repeat signup. Joining the Google Group alone does not enroll you in the Play closed test.

Join the closed test on Google Play
Previously joined internal testing?

If a page still identifies your enrollment as internal testing, use the old link below to leave that program, then return to step 2. Already enrolled in the closed beta? Stay enrolled.

Open the old internal test to leave it

Old link says “App not available”? Internal access has been removed, so that link may no longer offer a Leave button. Skip it and open the main testing link in step 2. An unavailable old link does not prove whether you are enrolled in the closed beta.

Keep WildStat installed while switching. Google’s changes can take several hours. If the closed test is unavailable, check that all three Google accounts match and retry later.

If the main testing page is still unavailable or still identifies you as an internal tester, send the exact message or a screenshot to support@wildstatmmo.com. Do not repeatedly leave and rejoin a working closed test.

3. Install or update WildStat

After opting in, use Google Play to install or update the game.

Open WildStat in Google Play

Please play, report bugs, and remain opted in for at least 14 continuous days. Google requires at least 12 closed testers to stay opted in for that period before we can apply for public release. Installing the game or joining the Group alone does not satisfy this requirement.

Send feedback to support@wildstatmmo.com or use /bug in the game.
